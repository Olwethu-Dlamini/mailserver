What Others Do

I am not the first person to put a mail server behind CGNAT, or to bridge Cloudflare Email Routing to a box at home. This page records what other people built for the same problem, what their projects do better than mine, and what each one gives me to take.

Collected on 12 October 2026 from GitHub, Cloudflare's issue tracker and docs, and Stalwart's docs and support forum. Stars and dates are as shown on the pages that day. Anything I could not open is marked unverified. Sources are listed in notes/others-2026-10-12.md.

0. The finding that changed part 10

Part 10 hoped Cloudflare's ARC headers would carry its SPF verdict to Stalwart. workerd issue #6740, open since May 2026 with no reply from Cloudflare, shows that mail handed to an Email Worker has no Authentication-Results, no Received or Received-SPF headers, and arc=none. The connecting IP is never exposed. So on this design SPF cannot be checked anywhere after Cloudflare, by anyone. The honest fix is the one Stalwart's maintainer recommends for relays: stop checking SPF on the tunnel's listener and let DKIM and DMARC decide. Part 10 now says that.

The same issue shows one useful header that does arrive: x-cf-spamh-score, Cloudflare's own spam score for the message.

3. Stalwart practice from its docs and forum

  • Relays and SPF. A June 2026 support thread, answered by the maintainer, covers servers behind an inbound relay: use listener or remote_ip in the sender-authentication expressions to set SPF, DKIM and DMARC checks to disable or relaxed for relay traffic. Use PROXY protocol where the relay supports it, and lock the relay listener down.
  • Auto-ban. The docs give defaults of 35 abuse events and 100 failed logins a day, and with no ban period the ban never expires. The docs show no allow-list and do not say whether localhost is exempt. On this design every inbound message shares one address, so this is the single setting most likely to stop all mail at once. Stalwart issue #3326 reports bans after the first expiry being logged but not enforced (not inspected).
  • Backups. The supported method from the forum (thread #386, May 2026) is: stop Stalwart, copy the whole /var/lib/stalwart directory, start it. The --export and --import commands also need the server stopped, and the docs say they are "not a substitute for proper backup procedures".
  • Metrics. Stalwart has a built-in Prometheus endpoint at /metrics/prometheus and an OpenTelemetry push option. The stock Grafana dashboard (23498) broke when metric names changed; Souheib-h/stalwart-monitoring-stack has a corrected one but no alert rules.
  • Report analysis. Stalwart analyses incoming DMARC, TLS-RPT and failure reports itself and logs problems as events. With rua already pointing at postmaster@, that may be enough without a separate parser.
  • MTA-STS behind Cloudflare. My MX is Cloudflare's, so my MTA-STS policy must list Cloudflare's MX hosts. sethCHN/mta-sts-proxy is a Worker that serves Cloudflare's published policy unchanged from mta-sts. on your own domain.

6. What I would take, in order

  1. Disable SPF on the tunnel's listener and let DKIM and DMARC decide (part 10, section 1).
  2. Check the auto-ban list today, set a ban period, and exempt the tunnel.
  3. Add the message.forward() fallback to the Worker; later, store-and-queue through R2 and Queues as mailbridge does.
  4. Back up by stop, copy, start, then restic off the VM, with a heartbeat to Uptime Kuma or healthchecks.io.
  5. Run an end-to-end canary from outside the network that alerts when it misses.
  6. Use x-cf-spamh-score as a spam signal in Sieve.
  7. Serve MTA-STS mirroring Cloudflare's policy, and turn on Stalwart's report analysis.
  8. If the cap or the Junk problem persists: a cheap VPS with WireGuard, HAProxy and PROXY protocol. Short of that, a second free relay (Mailjet's 200 a day is the verified next step) beside Resend.

Glossary for this part

PROXY protocol
A one-line header a proxy sends at the start of a TCP connection naming the real client IP and port, so the server behind it sees the true sender instead of the proxy.
Store and forward (Worker)
Writing each inbound message to durable storage (R2) and a queue before trying to deliver it, so delivery can be retried after an outage.
x-cf-spamh-score
A header Cloudflare Email Routing adds to messages it passes to a Worker, carrying its own spam score. One of the few signals about the real sender that survives the trip.
Heartbeat monitor
A check that expects a job to report in on a schedule and alerts when it goes quiet. Suits backups and canaries, which succeed silently.
